\section{Numbers in running text}

In units of one minimally coupled scalar, the kinetic parts sum to \smKinetic{} and the
curvature insertions to \smInsertion{}, a net of \smNet{}. The count is positive exactly
when the insertion's weighting exceeds \smThreshold{}~$\approx$~\smThresholdDecimal{};
the cutoff gives six, a margin of \smMarginPct{}. Entanglement \smEntanglement{} and the
contact term \smContact{} give the same net. With one doublet the count runs
\genCountZero, \genCountOne, \genCountTwo{} and \genCountThree{} for zero to three
generations, and attraction bounds the Higgs doublet's common coupling by
$\xi<{}$\smCouplingBound. The Standard Model needs a cutoff from \smCutoffLow{} to
\smCutoffHigh{}\,$M_P$. Citations resolve:
\cite{Sakharov1967,BrodaSzanecki2009,Tanaka1996,Kabat1995}.
